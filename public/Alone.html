ESSENTIALS

• Move: WASD or Arrow Keys

• Hotbar: Scroll or press number keys to select an item.

• Tools: Selected tools harvest, fight, and lose durability with use.

• Torch: Lasts 1 minute while selected at Night.

• Click a tree, item or creature to walk to it and use it. No axe? Clicking a tree shakes it (3 times a day).

• Hold SPACE to auto-gather: pick up, chop and shake what's nearby.

• Boats: click a boat to board it, then move to shore and press SPACE to leave it. Click a 🪧 sign to write on it.

• Hold F to fight the nearest creature (Axe, Pickaxe, or Sword). WASD takes control back.
