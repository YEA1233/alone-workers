FunMAJORNovember 16, 2026

ALONE to celebrate its 1 year birthday in January

  • ALONE's roots lie in December 2025. It was originally a game called 'ZOMBZ Survival'. Many know this, but ZOMBZ is not the original roots of ALONE!
  • It truly lies in this random, seemingly unrelated project named cIRC. No, it's not an internet relay client. cIRC was a chatting apoplication and the first web app I ever made. People on there said I should make a game so that's what I did.
  • ALONE is a labor of love that has evolved significantly since its inception. In January next year, I'm going to make a comprehensive history of the game and all of my projects in computers in general as the first thing I truly made was actually in January 2024.
Bosses · World tokens MAJORNovember 16, 2026

Boss tokens are world treasures

  • The Alpha Wolf now summons six wolves at a time and resumes summoning immediately after a five-second stun.
  • Greg drops one Ivory Token and the Alpha Wolf drops one Wolf Token per world. Pick them up from the ground to add them to the world's hidden shared token collection; a unique animation and sound plays on pickup.
Crafting · Combat MAJORNovember 16, 2026

Shields and Greg's ivory trophy

  • Craft a wooden shield (225 durability, 60% damage reduction) or an iron shield (600 durability, 80% reduction). Click a shield in your inventory to equip it in the separate shield slot beside your hotbar.
  • Greg now drops an ivory shield with 1,500 durability that blocks 90% of incoming damage. Shields lose durability based on the damage they absorb.
Bosses · Wildlife MAJORNovember 16, 2026

The Alpha Wolf hunts after dark

  • The 1,250-HP Alpha Wolf begins appearing on nights around days 9–12 and vanishes at dawn. If it survives the night, it returns nightly until defeated.
  • Every seven seconds it summons a pack of stronger wolves. Defeat its summoned wolves to stun it for 10 seconds and open a damage window. “You're Cooked” plays while the Alpha Wolf is nearby.
  • Creative tools can summon the Alpha Wolf for testing and toggle invulnerability.
Music · Greg MINORNovember 16, 2026

New soundtrack and Greg's defeat

  • Music now fades between boss, combat, and work tracks. A looping menu soundtrack plays through the main menu, submenus, and pause screen. Work includes chopping trees, breaking rocks, placing structures, and dismantling them; work actions refresh the music timer. Cave work and all combat tracks can replay without a cooldown.
  • Defeating Greg starts a brief shake before he fades away. His reward chest fades in afterward alongside the new “Too easy” soundtrack.
Seasons · Survival MAJORNovember 13, 2026

Winter arrives in the wilds

  • Seasons now change every 7–10 days. In winter, body heat drops quickly away from campfires and held torches, and freezing causes rapid damage. The blue screen tint grows stronger as you freeze.
  • Deserts stay summer-warm, restore your body heat, and have no winter tint. There is no warmth meter to watch.
  • Craft a thermal stone to carry warmth farther. It heats near a campfire or torch, cools while away from heat, and can be warmed in a chest beside a campfire. Cold stones make you freeze faster.
Achievements · Characters MAJORNovember 8, 2026

Achievements and unlockable characters

  • Earn achievements for surviving, building, exploring caves, defeating Greg, hosting a busy world, and even dying. Progress is saved to your browser profile.
  • Some achievements unlock new character emojis. Open Achievements to see goals and progress, or choose a character to browse unlocked and locked emojis.
Multiplayer · Building MINOROctober 30, 2026

Shared chests and no more structure lock-in

  • Open multiplayer chests now refresh as the server updates them. Your inventory and the chest stay in sync while you move items, including changes made by other players.
  • Structure ownership has been removed. Any player with a hammer can dismantle structures, including walls that could otherwise trap someone. Chests must still be emptied first, and dismantling still returns some materials.
Camera · World rendering MAJOROctober 28, 2026

First-person mode gets a wider, steadier view

  • Mouse look and camera-relative movement work in solo and multiplayer. Multiplayer now predicts movement locally and smoothly corrects toward the server position to reduce camera jitter.
  • The view uses a wider 90° horizontal field of view. The camera follows the elevation of the tile beneath you, and nearby players and world sprites are grounded to their terrain.
  • Night and caves use a separate darkness overlay with light sources, while water sits below land with filled shore edges to hide gaps.

Worlds · Multiplayer MAJOROctober 26, 2026

More ways to start and keep a world

  • Solo and hosted worlds have interchangeable save slots. Host a saved world, create a public or private server, or join from the server browser.
  • The same browser character can return with its position and inventory. Maps persist between sessions, are shared across the server, and show other players.
  • The official public world stays listed and pauses when empty. Admin tools include player-targeted creative access, OHKO, and first-person controls.
Caves · Exploration MAJOROctober 26, 2026

A world beneath the surface

  • World generation can disable caves. When enabled, large cave entrances lead to connected underground exits, marked on the map.
  • Caves are dark except near skylights and the brightly lit Greg arena. They contain more rock and iron; surface mountain biomes have fewer rocks and no iron.
  • Generation now reports its progress, and cave layouts include winding passages and connected chambers.
Crafting · Light MINOROctober 26, 2026

Lanterns and amber

  • Lanterns shine brighter than torches and last eight minutes. Amber can refuel a lantern.
  • Use the extractor with at least five wood selected: each attempt consumes five wood and can produce one, two, or three amber.
Bosses · Wildlife MAJOROctober 26, 2026

Greg from Accounting arrives underground

  • Greg is a 2,000-HP elephant boss who charges. Evade the charge and attack while he is stunned; the fight is built for a group but can be attempted solo.
  • Greg stays in a 20×20-tile arena with surrounding illumination. When he first targets someone, the arena seals until he is defeated or no players remain inside.
  • Defeating Greg turns elephants hostile at close range and spawns a reward chest with supplies, healing food, amber, and durable equipment.
Building · Survival MINOROctober 26, 2026

Tools, ruins, and survival tuning

  • The hammer lets you walk up to and dismantle a structure; empty chests first. Ruin frequency is adjustable, and ruin chests can contain larger supplies and worn tools.
  • Big rocks yield more stone, shovels are easier to craft, and wolves are less overwhelming and leave at dawn. Trees can also be shaken for occasional drops.
  • Click-to-walk helps approach targets; hold Space to gather and F to attack.

ESSENTIALS

• Move: WASD or Arrow Keys

• Hotbar: Scroll or press number keys to select an item.

• Tools: Selected tools harvest, fight, and lose durability with use.

• Torch: Lasts 1 minute while selected at Night.

• Click a tree, item or creature to walk to it and use it. No axe? Clicking a tree shakes it (3 times a day).

• Hold SPACE to auto-gather: pick up, chop and shake what's nearby.

• Winter: the seasons change every 7–10 days. Campfires and held torches keep you warm; the blue tint strengthens as you freeze. Deserts stay summer-warm. A carried thermal stone stores heat near a fire and helps you stay warm; a cold stone makes you freeze faster.

• Boats: click a boat to board it, then move to shore and press SPACE to leave it. Click a 🪧 sign to write on it.

• Hold F to fight the nearest creature (Axe, Pickaxe, or Sword). WASD takes control back.
